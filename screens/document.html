Dear Shri Ramesh Malhotra,

I, Arjun Kumar, residing at the above-mentioned address, through this legal notice, hereby call upon you to immediately refund the security deposit of ₹80,000 (Rupees Eighty Thousand Only) which was paid by me at the time of commencement of tenancy of your property situated at Flat 301, Vijay Society, Bandra West, Mumbai, and which you have wrongfully withheld despite my vacating the premises on 12 April 2026.

The facts of the matter are as follows: I entered into a registered rental agreement with you on 10 April 2023 for a monthly rent of ₹22,000, and paid a refundable security deposit of ₹80,000 as confirmed by NEFT Transfer Reference No. HDFC2023041000823 dated 11 April 2023. I vacated the premises on 12 April 2026 after completing the agreed notice period of 30 days, as acknowledged by you in our WhatsApp exchange dated 14 March 2026.

Under Clause 7 of our Rental Agreement dated 10 April 2023, the security deposit was to be refunded within 30 days of vacant possession, after deducting legitimate and documented charges for damage, if any. As of this date, 45 days have elapsed and you have neither refunded the deposit nor provided any itemized deduction statement, constituting a clear breach of contractual obligation.

Your conduct constitutes a deficiency in service within the meaning of Section 2(11) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and also amounts to unfair trade practice under Section 2(47) of the said Act. You are further liable under Sections 73 and 74 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 for breach of the rental agreement.

Demands Under This Notice

  1. Immediately refund the full security deposit of ₹80,000 within 15 days of receipt of this notice.
  2. Pay interest at 12% per annum on the said amount for the period of wrongful withholding (45 days to date), amounting to approximately ₹1,315.
  3. If deductions are claimed, provide a written itemized statement with supporting bills or estimates within 15 days — failing which no deduction shall be permissible.
Take notice that if you fail to comply with the above demands within fifteen (15) days from the date of receipt of this notice, I shall be constrained, without any further notice, to initiate appropriate proceedings before the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, Mumbai, seeking refund of the deposit together with compensation for harassment, legal costs, and such other relief as the Commission may deem fit. This may also expose you to a penalty under Section 88 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

This notice is being sent without prejudice to all other rights and remedies available to me in law. The cost and consequences of ignoring this notice shall be entirely at your peril.
