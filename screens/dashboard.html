Opponent Intelligence

What your landlord will argue.

01"You damaged the flat — I'm deducting from the deposit."
▾
Their Argument
Claims routine wear-and-tear is "damage." Common tactic, rarely documented.
Why It Might Work
If no move-out inspection report exists, courts sometimes allow partial deductions.
How Our Notice Pre-empts It
Demands itemized damage report in 15 days. No report = no deduction. Sharma v. Mehta (2022, Pune CF).
02"You left early — deposit covers remaining rent."
▾
Their Argument
Deposit offsets rent if notice period not served without mutual consent.
Why It Might Work
Courts uphold if lease specifies notice period and tenant vacated without written consent.
How Our Notice Pre-empts It
Your WhatsApp messages show landlord acknowledged exit date — implied consent. Notice cites this exchange.
03"There was no formal deposit — it was an advance."
▾
Their Argument
Reclassifies payment as "advance rent," not refundable under same provisions.
Why It Might Work
Ambiguous agreement language like "advance" creates legal room for dispute.
How Our Notice Pre-empts It
Bank transfer + agreement both use "deposit." Courts look at substance, not labelling. Consumer Forum Mumbai 2023.
