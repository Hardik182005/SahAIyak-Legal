Opponent Intelligence

What your landlord will argue.

01"You damaged the flat — I'm deducting from the deposit."
▾
Their Argument
Claims routine wear-and-tear is "damage." Common tactic, rarely documented.
Why It Might Work
If no move-out inspection report exists, courts sometimes allow partial deductions.
How Our Notice Pre-empts It
Demands itemized damage report in 15 days. No report = no deduction. Sharma v. Mehta (2022, Pune CF).
02"You left early — deposit covers remaining rent."
▾
Their Argument
Deposit offsets rent if notice period not served without mutual consent.
Why It Might Work
Courts uphold if lease specifies notice period and tenant vacated without written consent.
How Our Notice Pre-empts It
Your WhatsApp messages show landlord acknowledged exit date — implied consent. Notice cites this exchange.
03"There was no formal deposit — it was an advance."
▾
Their Argument
Reclassifies payment as "advance rent," not refundable under same provisions.
Why It Might Work
Ambiguous agreement language like "advance" creates legal room for dispute.
How Our Notice Pre-empts It
Bank transfer + agreement both use "deposit." Courts look at substance, not labelling. Consumer Forum Mumbai 2023.

Court Filing Limitation Period
3 Years from Cause of Action
Under the Limitation Act, 1963, civil claims must be filed within 3 years from when the dispute first arose. Delaying beyond this limit may bar your claim entirely unless delay condonation is formally requested and accepted by the court.
Step-by-Step Filing Guide
1
Send the legal notice via Registered Post (AD)
Use the notice we drafted. Send via Registered Post with Acknowledgement Due. Keep the postal receipt and the acknowledgement card when it comes back — the court will ask for both. Wait 15 days for the opponent's response.
2
Prepare three sets of documents
Print 3 copies of: (a) the filled Consumer Complaint Form, (b) all your evidence — WhatsApp screenshots, agreements, NEFT receipts, (c) the legal notice + postal receipt. Staple each set separately. Attach a ₹200 court fee stamp purchased from any post office.
3
File at the District Consumer Forum counter
Visit the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission between 10am and 4pm on a working day. Submit your 3 sets at the filing counter. You will receive an acknowledgement slip with your unique complaint number — keep this safe.
4
Attend the first hearing (usually within 21 days)
The forum issues a notice to your opponent. If they fail to appear, the forum may decide ex-parte (without them) in your favour. In deposit disputes, most opponents settle at this stage after receiving a formal notice from the court.
5
Enforce the order if needed
Consumer Forum orders are enforceable like civil court decrees. If the opponent ignores the order, file an Execution Petition — the forum can direct attachment of their bank account or property. This step is rarely needed; most cases end at hearing 1 or 2.
