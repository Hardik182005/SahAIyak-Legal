India has 1.4 billion people and 4.5 crore pending court cases. Most people don't fight because they don't know how. We're fixing that — one case at a time.

01 — The Problem

The law is written for everyone. Access is reserved for the few.

In India, 80% of citizens cannot afford a lawyer. The average single consultation costs ₹5,000 — more than many earn in a week. Legal aid offices are understaffed, underfunded, and inaccessible in most towns.

So ordinary people — a tenant cheated of a deposit, a worker denied salary, a consumer sold a faulty product — give up. Not because their case is weak. Because the system is designed for those who can afford to navigate it.

02 — Our Belief

Knowing your rights should cost nothing.

We built SahAIyak on a simple belief: if you have a legitimate grievance under Indian law, you deserve to know it — clearly, immediately, and in your own language. You deserve to know your odds of winning before you spend a rupee. You deserve a legal notice that is yours, drafted in 60 seconds, not 10 days.

This is not charity. This is the product that should have been built ten years ago.

03 — What We Are Not

Not a chatbot. Not a template generator. Not legal advice.

SahAIyak is a legal intelligence engine. It reads 10,000+ real Indian court judgments, extracts patterns, and applies them to your specific facts. The difference is the difference between a library and a lawyer.

We are not lawyers. We are not a law firm. We do not provide legal advice. What we provide is the intelligence to understand your rights, the courage to exercise them, and the tools to start the process — for free.

Our Principles
01

Free for individuals. Always.

Every Indian citizen gets full access to case analysis, win prediction, and legal notice drafting — free, forever. We charge enterprises, legal firms, and government bodies for scale. The individual never pays. This is non-negotiable.

02

Local first. Hindi, Marathi, English.

An AI that only works in English serves 15% of India. We support voice and text input in Hindi, Marathi, and English — with Tamil, Telugu, and Bengali next. Justice is not a translation problem we're willing to ignore.

03

Real data. No hallucinations.

Every probability is grounded in real cases from real Indian courts. We cite our sources. Every legal notice references actual sections of actual laws. We are accountable to facts, not impressions. If we don't know, we say so.

04

Built with, not just for.

We work with legal aid lawyers, consumer rights advocates, and grassroots organisations. The product is built with real user feedback from real people who faced real injustice. Not in a conference room. In the field.
